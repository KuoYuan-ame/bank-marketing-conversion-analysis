# Bank Marketing: Contact Frequency Analysis

## Data validation
- Records: 45,211; subscriptions: 5,289; overall rate: 11.70%.
- Q3/Q4 outputs reconcile to the same totals.

## Findings
- 1-contact subscription rate: 14.60%.
- 11+-contact subscription rate: 3.93%.
- Within each housing-loan group, observed subscription rates decrease across the four contact-frequency bands.

## Limitations
- Observational associations are not causal effects of adding calls.
- No unique customer identifier is available; these are record-level metrics.
- Contact counts are observed at the end of a campaign and are subject to selection and reverse causality.
